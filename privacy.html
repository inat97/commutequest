🧭Commute Quest

Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no server of ours.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Your places, routes and trips stay on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • To show the weather, the app asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) about one location per trip.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Optional: your home and work places (latitude, longitude, a 150 m radius and a label you can see), only if you save them.
  • For each finished trip: start and end time, direction, how you travelled, the weather, distance, and the map cells (hexagons about 150 m wide) you passed through.
  • Your map, village, materials, companions, streak and settings.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Widgets and the Live Activity read a short summary on the same device.

Location

Location is optional; the game also works with manual buttons. If you save home and work, iOS watches those two circles and tells the app when you leave or arrive. Between those two moments, the app records your route in the background (iOS shows the blue location indicator). Each point is turned into a map cell on your iPhone, rotated by an angle unique to your device, and the point itself is discarded when the trip ends. No location ever goes to us.

Motion

When a trip ends, the app asks iOS whether you were walking, cycling or riding during that time, to pick your hero's role. This motion data is read on your iPhone and only the result (for example "cycling") is kept.

Weather

Once per trip, the app asks Apple's WeatherKit for the current weather at your destination. Apple receives that location to answer the request, under Apple's privacy policy; Apple says it does not use WeatherKit requests to identify you. We do not receive the request or the answer.

Notifications

"Your journal is ready" notifications are created on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Commute Quest+ is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether a subscription is active.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's data is included in that backup like any other app, under Apple's privacy policy. We have no copy. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we collect no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete — it is all on your device and under your control. In the app, Settings › Start over erases your progress, and removing the places deletes the saved locations. Questions are still welcome: developers9x@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change. If the app ever starts sending data to us, this page will say so first and the app will ask you.

This page describes the app as built. It is not legal advice.
