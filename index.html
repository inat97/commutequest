🧭Commute Quest

Your commute is an expedition.

Commute Quest is a game played with your real trip to work. Leave home and your hero sets out. Arrive and a short journal and a chest are waiting. Rain and traffic jams pay better.

How it works

  • Set out with one tap, or automatically when you leave home or work (if you save both places and allow location "Always").
  • Put the phone away. There is nothing to do while you travel. If the phone moves at car speed, the app covers itself until you stop.
  • Arrive and read a 4–7 page journal written from your real trip: how you travelled, the weather, delays and new streets. Then open the chest.
  • Build your village with what you bring home, and earn 12 companions by things you really do.

Help

Trips don't start by themselves. In the app: Settings › Places, save both Home and Work, then allow location "Always". iOS may take a minute to notice you left. You can always tap "Set out" instead.

Restore Commute Quest+ on a new iPhone. Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel Commute Quest+. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Commute Quest+. Apple handles billing and refunds: request a refund from Apple.

Stop location use. In the app: Settings › Places, remove both places. Or turn off Location for Commute Quest in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Move or back up your progress. Your map, village and companions live only on your iPhone. They are included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup; there is no Commute Quest account or server to restore from.
